# Neighbors — charter

**Purpose.** Get people to use the app, and through using it enrich their communities through sharing — where sharing means sharing *information*, never opinions.

**The test for every feature.** It ships only if it still works when nothing a user types is ever shown to another user.

## Rules that do not change

1. **Never a marketplace.** No price field exists in the data model. No business or organization accounts (sources are feeds, not members). No payments, ever.
2. **No voice.** No comments, replies, upvotes, rankings, friend lists, streaks or leaderboards. Contributions are structured: a feed URL, an item with an official link, a confirm or a flag.
3. **Official sources only.** Every item links to a government, school, library or recognized nonprofit page. An item with no such link is not rejected for being bad; it is rejected for being unverifiable.
4. **Nothing collected.** No account, no GPS, no analytics. The ZIP stays on the phone. The server keeps a push token and a rate-limit counter and nothing else.
5. **Never compete with an official alert.** The phone already receives Wireless Emergency Alerts. This app is where a person looks afterward for the details and the official link.

## Categories (the fence)

governance · elections · public-spaces · public-services · volunteering · public-safety

If an item fits none of these, it does not belong here.

## Contribution

"Help your neighbors stay informed." Submit a source, submit an item, confirm, flag, or suggest an idea. Submissions are held until the link resolves on an allowed domain and a review passes; a public **What's new** page credits what landed ("a neighbor added the Fairfax library calendar"). Ideas go in privately; outcomes come out publicly.
